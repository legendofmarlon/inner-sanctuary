# 🌿 Inner Sanctuary Companion & Grant Clipper (Chrome / Edge Extension)

Official browser extension for **Inner Sanctuary Wellness Foundation** (IRS 501(c)(3) Public Charity | EIN: 39-3757168).

## Features:
1. **🎯 1-Click Grant Clipper:** Scrapes title, funding amounts, and deadlines from any web page and formats them for your Zoho Bigin CRM pipeline and Google Drive dossiers.
2. **📋 1-Click 501(c)(3) Auto-Fill Drawer:** Instantly copies EIN (`39-3757168`), legal tax status, leadership names (Teshia Campbell & Marlon Smith), 100-word mission abstracts, and somatic methodology statements directly into web grant applications.
3. **⚡ Instant Shortcuts:** One-click navigation to Gemini Notebook (NotebookLM), Zoho Bigin CRM, Google Drive Knowledge Base, and the official website.

---

## 🚀 How to Install in Chrome or Edge (15 Seconds):

1. Open your browser and go to:
   * Chrome: `chrome://extensions`
   * Edge: `edge://extensions`
2. Toggle on **Developer mode** in the top-right corner.
3. Click the **Load unpacked** button in the top-left.
4. Select the folder:
   `C:\Users\danie\OneDrive\Documents\GitHub\inner-sanctuary\extension`
5. Click the puzzle piece icon in your Chrome toolbar and **Pin** the Inner Sanctuary emblem to your toolbar!
