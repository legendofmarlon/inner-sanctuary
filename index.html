Our Sacred Purpose

Our Mission

✦ IRS 501(c)(3) Public Charity • EIN: 39-3757168 • Teshia Campbell, Founder • Marlon Smith, President
✦

Inner Sanctuary Wellness Foundation creates welcoming community experiences that support emotional wellness, self-awareness, authentic expression, and meaningful human connection.

Through reflection, creativity, and shared experiences, we help people tune into themselves, feel witnessed and understood, and cultivate a greater sense of belonging—within themselves and with others.

Our Philosophy

The Sanctuary Begins Within.

Inner Sanctuary isn't somewhere we go. It's something we cultivate within ourselves.

It's the space where we learn to listen to our own experiences, meet ourselves with compassion, and feel at home in who we are.

And while that journey begins within, we don't have to make it alone. Through reflection, creativity, and connection, we create opportunities to nurture that sanctuary together.

Inner Sanctuary works through three intentional practices that guide every gathering, activation, and experience.

🌱

TUNE IN

Through reflection and intentional awareness, we practice noticing our thoughts, emotions, and needs without judgment.

EXPRESS

Through music, poetry, storytelling, creativity, and movement, we create room for authentic expression.

✦ Pillar 2 • Creative Release
