PAUSE. BREATHE. REMEMBER.

There was a time when we knew exactly who we were.

Before the world told us who to become. Before we learned to silence our emotions, shrink our expression, and search outside ourselves for the peace we once carried within.

Somewhere between surviving, striving, and trying to belong, we forgot.

We forgot how to listen to ourselves. How to feel without judgment. How to simply be.

✦ But what if the essence of who we are was never lost? ✦

What if it's been there all along, waiting for us to remember?

At Inner Sanctuary Wellness Foundation, we help people tune into the essence of who they are.
We create spaces to pause, breathe, explore the elements and emotions within, and find our way back to our roots, our origin, our true selves.

Somatic Alchemy & Nature’s Prescription

Our Medicine? LSD.

*(No, not that kind of LSD, silly!)* 😄
✦
LAUGHING • SINGING • DANCING

Before anyone calls the wellness authorities... around here, healing isn't clinical, stiff, or complicated. Sometimes healing looks like deep belly laughter that shakes your ribs until you're gasping for air. A song you didn't know you needed to belt out. Or moving your body freely until whatever you've been holding onto finally shakes loose.

Our medicine is simply giving ourselves permission to be human.
To feel it all, express it all, and remember who we were before the rush.

L

Laughing

Nervous System Reset

Shared belly joy that instantly dissolves fight-or-flight survival mode, releases endorphins, and reminds us that lightness is our natural state.

✦ Joy Medicine
S

Singing

Vibrational Sound Release

Sounding your sacred voice and giving breath to frequencies words alone cannot carry. No choir training needed; just raw, unfiltered vocal resonance.

✦ Voice Medicine
D

Dancing

Embodied Somatic Motion

Moving energy freely without choreography, performance anxiety, or permission. Shaking off heavy weeks and reconnecting your root back to soil.

✦ Motion Medicine
🕊️ And don't worry — we hold plenty of space for the rest of being human, too: the tears (Crying), authentic belonging (Connecting), and honest storytelling (Creating). Whatever is in your heart, the circle holds.
The Foundation Truth

"Because the sanctuary we've been searching for isn't a place we have to find."

It's a place we learn to create within ourselves. And when we do, we can carry that sanctuary wherever life takes us.

Theory of Change & Logic Model

Community Health Equity & Impact Architecture

How our somatic and creative safe space interventions translate into measurable resilience, stress reduction, and grassroots community stability.

Step 01 • Target Need

The Burnout & Stigma Gap

High-stress environments, isolation, and lack of affordable, non-stigmatized, alcohol-free healing spaces for frontline community members and emerging artists.

  • 76% of emerging artists report acute stress & burnout
  • Prohibitive costs for private clinical somatic therapy
  • Scarcity of substance-free creative sanctuaries
Step 02 • Somatic Intervention

Unhurried Somatic Spaces

Accessible, non-clinical community gatherings combining The Four Releases (Breath, Vocal Resonance, Embodied Motion, Rest) and Somatic Medicine (Laughing, Singing, Dancing).

  • Guided 4x4 Box Breathing & nervous system resets
  • Supportive Community Open Mic & expressive stages
  • Botanical tea sanctuary & peer connection circles
Step 03 • Measurable Outputs

Grassroots Delivery & Scale

Direct, quantifiable outputs documented at each gathering to verify equitable community benefit for grantmakers and CRA officers.

  • 100% Free or low-barrier access for all participants
  • 40+ Local BIPOC & emerging artists spotlit & paid
  • 500+ Hours of somatic regulation delivered annually
Step 04 • Long-Term Impact

Generational Healing Equity

Sustainable emotional self-regulation, reduced isolation, strengthened civic fabric, and a replicable model for grassroots public wellness.

  • 94% report immediate nervous system deceleration
  • Strengthened neighborhood resilience & trust
  • Scalable framework across Orange County & Inland Empire
