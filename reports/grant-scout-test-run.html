A. Executive Summary & Core Philosophy

Inner Sanctuary begins within. Inner Sanctuary Wellness Foundation (EIN: 39-3757168) creates welcoming community experiences that support emotional wellness, self-awareness, authentic expression, and meaningful human connection. Through reflection, creativity, and shared experiences, we help people tune into themselves, feel witnessed and understood, and cultivate a greater sense of belonging—within themselves and with others.

We operate an alcohol-free, trauma-informed sanctuary model with $0 cost barrier to entry. Our philosophy bridges classical somatic awareness ("Pause. Breathe. Remember.") with unhurried creative release ("Sometimes, Coming Home to Yourself Looks Like Joy — Laugh. Sing. Dance.").
