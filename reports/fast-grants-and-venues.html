2. Zero-Cost Venue Matcher Strategy

Public Libraries & Municipal Cultural Centers

In California, public library systems (OC Public Libraries, LAPL, Riverside County) and civic cultural centers grant community room fee waivers to registered 501(c)(3) non-profits offering free public wellness and arts education.

Mindful Tea Lounges & Yoga Studios

Conscious spaces have empty off-peak windows (Sunday evenings 6-9 PM, weekday mornings). Inner Sanctuary brings 25–50 respectful attendees who buy beverages and support the space with zero upfront rental costs.
