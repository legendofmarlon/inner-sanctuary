## 1. Institutional Overview & Mission Statement

Inner Sanctuary Wellness Foundation is a trauma-informed, community-grounded 501(c)(3) public charity dedicated to eradicating chronic mental wellness disparities, systemic isolation, and creative burnout. 

We deliver barrier-free third spaces anchored in acoustic artistry, mindfulness, and the Four Biological Releases (Laughing, Singing/Sounding, Dancing, and Crying). All community gatherings operate with zero ticket costs, 100% alcohol- and substance-free environments, and welcoming intergenerational fellowship.

## 4. Key Institutional Programs

1. **SoCal Creatives Collective Monthly Open Mics:** Intimate acoustic gatherings pairing grassroots poets, instrumentalists, and singers with attentive, respectful audiences.
2. **Permission to Pause Stations:** Mobile somatic resting stations equipped with tactile intention bracelet weaving, herbal teas, and sensory grounding prompts.
3. **The Innertunement Studio & Creative Archive:** Non-commercial storytelling, acoustic sound healing, and digital reflection spaces.
4. **Future Ecological Retreat Sanctuary:** Long-term capital vision securing 5–20 acres in Southern California for nature therapy, eco-cabins, and retreat healing.
