Institutional Overview

Inner Sanctuary Wellness Foundation is an IRS-recognized 501(c)(3) public charity dedicated to trauma-informed somatic wellness, substance-free mindful gatherings, and grassroots community healing across Orange County (Irvine, Santa Ana) and the Inland Empire (Riverside).

Co-founded by Executive Director Teshia Campbell and presided over by Marlon Smith, the foundation provides unhurried, non-clinical restorative spaces (*Permission to Pause*) where community members, emerging creatives, and neighbors can decompress, reflect, and belong.
