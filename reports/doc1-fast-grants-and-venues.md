## 2. Zero-Cost Venue Matcher Strategy

### A. Public Libraries & Municipal Cultural Centers
In California, public library systems (OC Public Libraries, LAPL, Riverside County) and civic cultural centers grant community room fee waivers to registered 501(c)(3) non-profits offering free public wellness, literature, and arts programming.

### B. Mindful Tea Lounges & Yoga Studios
Conscious spaces often have vacant off-peak windows (Sunday evenings 6:00 PM – 9:00 PM, weekday mornings). Inner Sanctuary brings 25 to 50 respectful, beverage-purchasing attendees who support the venue with zero upfront rental overhead.
