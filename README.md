# Inner Sanctuary Wellness Foundation — Landing Page

A complete, production-ready website for **Inner Sanctuary Wellness Foundation**, built with pure HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript adhering strictly to the brand identity, typography, and community mission.

---

## 🎨 Brand Identity & Palette

| Color Name | Hex Code | Purpose / Placement |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm Sand / Cream** | `#F6F3EC` | Full page background & soft ambient radiance |
| **Deep Forest Green** | `#2B4436` | Primary headers, bold serif titles, structured blocks |
| **Sunburst Gold** | `#C59A3F` | Medallion borders, buttons, subheadings, accents |
| **Earth Charcoal** | `#1C1B18` | Crisp, readable body copy |
| **Botanical Sage** | `#5F7B6B` | Badges, pills, metadata & bullet points |

- **Header Typography**: *Cinzel* (all-caps with wide tracking: 0.12em–0.15em)
- **Body Typography**: *Montserrat* (clean, modern sans-serif)

---

## 🌟 Sections & Interactive Features

1. **Top Announcement Bar**: Highlights Inner Sanctuary as a 501(c)(3) space for raw authenticity, somatic healing, and radical self-expression.
2. **Hero & Emblem Medallion**: Features your real uploaded emblem framed by a rotating celestial dashed gold ring, the `HEALTH • HARMONY • PEACE` motto, and official 501(c)(3) verification badge.
3. **The Four Releases: Nature’s Original Medicine**:
   - Somatic regenerative releases: **Laughing**, **Singing & Sounding**, **Dancing**, and **Crying**.
   - Responsive 4-column / 2x2 grid with clean breathing room and elegant typography.
   - Grounded **Our Purpose** manifesto callout card: *"Laugh. Sing. Move. Release. Reclaim your freedom."*
4. **The $1 Community Campaign & Official Zeffy Portal**:
   - "Plant a Seed in the Sanctuary": Demonstrating that grassroots power holds true community.
   - Interactive donation amount selector ($1 Seed, $5 Friend, $10 Nourisher, $25 Pillar, or Custom).
   - Dynamic real-time community impact calculator.
   - Direct integration with Inner Sanctuary's official **Zeffy Donation Portal** (0% platform fees for 501(c)(3) charities).
   - "Give With Love" button launches Zeffy directly in a secure new tab while triggering a celebratory leaf & heart flourish on screen.
5. **Mission & 3 Pillars**:
   - Mental & Emotional Wellness (Unhurried stillness & pause)
   - Authentic Creative Expression (Raw roots without algorithmic pressure)
   - Intergenerational Community Care (Neighborhood trust & welcoming spaces)
   - Core Quote: *"Go easy on yourself. It's okay to pause. It's okay to put yourself first."*
6. **Dual Audience Pathways**:
   - *For Artists & Performers*: Come home to your craft, unhurried listeners, stage time.
   - *For Brands, Banks & Sponsors*: CRA-eligible community reinvestment, 100% tax-deductible (IRC 170).
7. **Creator Spotlight Hub & The 30-Second Video Blueprint**:
   - `0:00 – 0:10`: The Intro & Plug
   - `0:10 – 0:20`: The Reflection (Why an unhurried sanctuary matters)
   - `0:20 – 0:30`: The Outro (Tap in & show love)
   - 4-card recording specs (Framing, Lighting/Audio, Energy, Delivery).
8. **CRA Sponsorship Tiers**:
   - Tier 1: Raffle & In-Kind Donor ($5–$25 or gift cards)
   - Tier 2: Community Neighbor ($100 / event)
   - Tier 3: Presenting Showcase Partner ($500 / showcase — Flagship / CRA Partner)
9. **"Drop Into The Circle" Interactive Intake Form**:
   - Interactive 4-role switcher tabs (*Artist Spotlight*, *$1 Seed Donor*, *Sponsor / CRA*, *Raffle Donor*).
   - Form fields auto-update placeholders, role dropdown, and submit action.
   - Grounded toast confirmation.
10. **Rich Grounded Footer & Social Presence**:
    - Deep Forest Green theme, emblem, leadership (Teshia Campbell & Marlon Smith), Santa Ana address, and verified 501(c)(3) tax ID (EIN: 39-3757168).
    - Connected directly to the official foundation Instagram: [@ourinnersanctuary](https://www.instagram.com/ourinnersanctuary/).
