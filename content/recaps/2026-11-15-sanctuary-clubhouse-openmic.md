# Gathering Summary

On Friday evening, November 15, 2026, Inner Sanctuary Wellness Foundation opened the doors of Our Sanctuary Clubhouse to over 50 community members for an unhurried night of acoustic vulnerability, diaphragmatic release, and somatic grounding.

### Sacred Moments
- **diaphragmatic release:** The circle opened with collective breath synchronization, grounding participants into a parasympathetic state before the first word was spoken.
- **stage highlights:** Performers shared original poetry on healing from burnout, recovery, and intergenerational resilience. 
- **fiduciary impact:** 100% of community donations went directly toward honoring local artists and funding future substance-free spaces.
